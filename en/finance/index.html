    Raw data

    2Calculation layer

    →

    3Dashboard

    Measures displayed with a shared filter:
    • Five KPI cards and automatic findings
    • Five analytical charts
    • Project table and this raw-data table
    Measure definitions (formulas)
    Revenue = sum of progress invoices in the period
    Gross profit = revenue − direct project costs
    Operating profit = gross profit − HQ operating expenses
    Cash balance = opening balance + receipts − payments
    Receivables = invoices issued − amounts collected
    Days sales outstanding (DSO) = closing receivables ÷ period revenue × days in period
    Budget achievement = actual ÷ budget
    Financial progress = cumulative billed work ÷ contract value
